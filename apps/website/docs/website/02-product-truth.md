# Product truth and messaging

Status: requirements from the supplied product brief. Detailed demo behavior must be verified in the app before production use.

## Product definition

NirmanSite is a construction-management application created primarily for Indian builders and contractors. It acts as a daily construction companion for organising project information and site operations.

It is not a property-listing app, marketplace, generic corporate ERP, accounting application, construction company website, or primarily a CRM.

## Different ways of working

The website must represent all of these arrangements without implying a mandatory hierarchy:

| Arrangement | What the visual story must accommodate |
| --- | --- |
| Construction company | Several projects and people sharing responsibilities |
| Small builder | One project with direct personal involvement |
| Builder hiring contractors | Delegated site execution |
| Hands-on builder | One person covering builder, contractor, and supervisor responsibilities |
| Independent contractor | Own projects, workforce, expenses, and site operations |

Avoid a universal Builder → Contractor → Supervisor diagram with three required accounts. Responsibilities may overlap and the organisation's structure may differ.

## App users and workers

Builders, contractors, and supervisors/site team members operate the app according to their responsibilities and access.

Daily workers are managed records. They are not expected to have app accounts. Marketing visuals must show the responsible app user recording worker details, attendance, advances, and wage activity.

## The problem

Important site information is fragmented across registers, notebooks, diaries, WhatsApp, photos, calls, verbal instructions, memory, and separate attendance/payment records.

The promise is better organisation and visibility of recorded information. Do not suggest that phone calls, WhatsApp, or human communication disappear.

## Kharchi meaning

Kharchi is money already given to a worker as an advance against wages. For example, a contractor gives a worker ₹500, records the advance, and later accounts for it during wage settlement.

Show recording an existing payment. Do not imply that NirmanSite transfers money, that the worker submits an in-app request, or that a new approval process is part of the demonstrated flow unless verified and explicitly in scope.

## Financial clarity

An important story is the movement from recording information to understanding information. Expenses should demonstrate supported amounts, categories, project totals, and period summaries.

Do not infer accounting, profit, cash balance, tax compliance, bank transfers, automatic savings, or forecasting from expense records. Illustrative amounts must be coherent and identifiable as demo data.

## Languages and usability

English, Hindi, and Gujarati support are meaningful product value. Show authentic translated app screens and maintain readable script typography.

The website's own translation scope remains open; app multilingual support does not automatically mean every website page has been commissioned in three languages.

Users may be moving around sites, using a phone outdoors, and unfamiliar with complex business software. Present a simple, clear, fast, visual, approachable experience.

## Founder story and proof

The origin story is that real conversations revealed different working arrangements, and the product was shaped around them. Do not invent interview quotes, participant counts, dates, testimonials, or measurable outcomes.

Before publishing a claim, identify the supporting screen, contract, supplied evidence, or founder-approved fact. Configuration-dependent and “where supported” capabilities must remain qualified until checked.
