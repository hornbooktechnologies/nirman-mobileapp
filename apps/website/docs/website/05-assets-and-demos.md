## Capture rules

- Use a consistent demo organisation, projects, worker identities, and coherent amounts.
- Use synthetic or appropriately cleared data. Exclude private phone numbers, credentials, personal notifications, and unrelated account information.
- Capture actual supported actions using a stable build; record the build/date and source screen for future refreshes.
- Keep the same device proportions and crop convention across demonstrations.
- Remove idle time and loading pauses in editing without misrepresenting product behavior or timing-sensitive outcomes.
- Keep important labels and amounts large enough to read; use faithful detail crops when the full screen is too small.
- Avoid heavy cursor effects, instruction paragraphs, rapid taps, and tutorial-like narration.
- Use an authentic app recording or faithfully animated screenshots. Do not generate fake app text or unsupported screens.
- Keep source captures separate from edited delivery assets. Preserve supplied originals.

## Example Kharchi storyboard

Proposal for a short demonstration; exact screens must be checked before capture:

1. Establish that ₹500 has already been given to a worker.
2. Show the responsible app user recording that advance.
3. Show the saved record.
4. Cut to a verified wage view that explains how the recorded advance is accounted for.

Use distinct shots if these events occur at different times. Do not imply wage settlement automatically happens when an advance is saved. If the full story is unreadable within ten seconds, split it into two brief demonstrations.

## Delivery format

- Prefer optimised MP4/WebM video over GIF for real recordings; choose encodings after browser/device tests.
- Use a sharp, representative poster image with known dimensions for each clip.
- Target approximately 5–10 seconds per focused demo; hero roughly 6–10 seconds. These are editorial targets, not forced limits.
- Provide muted inline playback; retain a pause/replay control. Audio must be intentional and user-initiated, with captions when needed.
- Pause outside the viewport or when the tab is hidden. Respect reduced motion and avoid downloading all videos at initial page load.
- On slow connections or playback failure, keep a useful poster and surrounding benefit copy visible.
- Use responsive images; crop for mobile deliberately. Final file-size budgets follow an asset test, not an unsupported performance claim.
