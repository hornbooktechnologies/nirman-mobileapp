## How to interpret this documentation

- **Requirement**: explicitly stated by the user in the planning conversation.
- **Working decision**: recommended direction accepted as the basis for planning; may be refined through visual review.
- **Proposal**: a concrete suggestion awaiting design or product validation.
- **Open**: information or a choice that is still needed.

The user-provided product and design briefs are the authority for marketing intent. These documents consolidate those briefs and subsequent discussion. They do not certify every feature's current runtime behavior; verify the relevant app screen and contract before recording demonstrations or publishing detailed claims.

## Maintenance rules

Search readiness and the staging-to-live sequence are documented in [SEO and search launch](10-seo-and-search-launch.md).

- Read the soul and product truth before proposing a new composition or feature claim.
- Record changed decisions and their rationale in the delivery plan.
- Update the authoritative document instead of creating competing briefs.
- Keep proposed copy, illustrative demo data, actual product evidence, and published claims clearly distinguished.
- Record completed implementation and verification honestly; planning checklists are not evidence of completion.
- Preserve supplied brand assets and unrelated repository work.
- This documentation task does not authorize installation, deployment, or production data changes.
